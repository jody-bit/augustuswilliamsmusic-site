Biography

Augustus Williams (Gusto) is a Detroit techno artist, DJ, and live-hardware performer with more than two decades in the city's underground. A Metroplex recording artist signed to Juan Atkins' foundational label, he builds floor-driven techno in real time from drum machines and sequencers — moving fluidly between DJ, hybrid, and full live-hardware sets. His machine-driven sound has carried him from Detroit warehouses to the Movement Festival stages and a Detroit–Berlin Connection residency.

Metroplex recording artist, signed by Juan Atkins. Detroit live-hardware techno, 20 years deep.
