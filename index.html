Biography

Augustus Williams is a Detroit electronic artist, DJ, live-hardware performer, and Metroplex recording artist signed to Juan Atkins’ foundational label. With more than two decades in the city’s underground electronic-music community, he moves fluidly between DJ sets, hybrid performances, and full live-hardware sets.

Using drum machines, synthesizers, sequencers, and live arrangement, Augustus constructs floor-driven performances in real time. His work emphasizes rhythm, pressure, discipline, improvisation, and the evolving relationship between performer, machines, and dance floor.

His career includes Movement Festival appearances, Detroit-Berlin cultural-exchange projects, releases through Metroplex Records, membership in Detroit Techno Militia, and long-standing involvement with Techno Snob.
