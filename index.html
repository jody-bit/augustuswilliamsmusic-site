Augustus Williams (Gusto)

Detroit Techno / DJ + Hybrid Performance

Detroit, MI

Biography

Augustus Williams is a Detroit-based techno artist and hybrid performer exploring the intersections of digital production and analog synthesis. His work emerges from the city's legendary electronic music legacy, informed by decades of underground club culture and the meticulous craft of minimal techno.

Known for immersive live performances that combine DJ sets with instrumental layers, Williams crafts sonic environments that demand presence—audio-visual experiences designed for listening rooms and intimate venues rather than background rotation.
